# Echo Archer Design Bible

## Core Identity

Echo Archer is the fantasy archery adventure with the giant mountain fortress.
The fortress is the visual symbol of the game: an ancient cliff-carved stronghold later expanded by the Archer Guild with warm timber additions, banners, terraces, bridges, and visible history.

## World Style

- Zelda-style fantasy adventure, ancient mystery, handcrafted exploration.
- Colorful but believable; beautiful places can still feel dangerous.
- Every major place needs a memorable silhouette visible from a useful distance.
- Avoid generic medieval design, grimdark realism, cube houses, pyramid roofs, and placeholder geometry.

## Fortress Rules

- The mountain fortress should anchor the world composition and appear in sightlines from guild, trails, high places, and future map art.
- Use ancient stone foundations, golden guild wood, big beams, cliffside terraces, balconies, bridges, banners, and multi-generation towers.
- The entrance should feel legendary: circular platform, guild activation mechanism, and a “bigger on the inside” promise.

## Guild Symbol

Official symbol: a stylized eagle holding an arrow.

Use it on:
- guild banners
- signs and shields
- guild buildings
- maps and UI
- Master Archer armor

Keep the symbol simple enough to read from a distance.

## Architecture Rules

- Buildings use stone foundations, timber framing, visible beams, asymmetry, overhangs, porches, chimneys, and layered roofs.
- Important houses should not be identical.
- Roofs should feel constructed, with ridges, eaves, fascia, trim, and uneven handmade proportions.

## Hall of Arrows

The Hall of Arrows is a ceremonial chamber honoring generations of Master Archers.
It should use stone pillars, statues, banners, warm light, and legendary bow displays that visually grow with player progression.

## Master Archer Armor

Master Archer armor should read as heroic knight-archer gear:
- light armor, not heavy plate
- guild-gold trims
- strong cloak and shoulder silhouette
- eagle-and-arrow status mark
- prestigious but agile

## Landmark Philosophy

Every major landmark should answer: “What makes this location unforgettable?”
Prioritize:
1. Strong silhouettes
2. Memorable landmarks
3. Architectural storytelling
4. Visual identity
5. Exploration reward
